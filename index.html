AI Pair-Programming Process

How this portfolio design came to life with Antigravity AI

01

Typography & Palette Extraction

Analyzed a Figma screenshot to map font sizes (Lexend Regular from 39px to 10px) and custom white/black color palettes to CSS Custom Properties.

02

Button Scaling System

Created button variants (Large, Medium, Small) mapping to B2, B3, and Caption typography sizes, built for responsiveness and focus accessibility.

03

Organic Scattering Canvas

Designed an absolute-positioned floating hero grid. Balanced positions outwards to prevent central overlap, utilizing custom animation delay offsets to avoid synchronized movements.

04

Light Theme Optimization

Switched canvas background to 'white 0', adjusted text contrast, and replaced vector assets with high-fidelity local brand logos.
